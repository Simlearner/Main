🏁 SLR Mission Statement

SimLearnerRacing (SLR) was founded on the belief that racing is more than a sport — it is a lifestyle that brings people together through passion, competition, and ambition.

SLR provides coaching for drivers of all skill levels, from hobby racers to elite alien-level competitors, alongside professionally managed servers for Assetto Corsa Competizione and upcoming support for Assetto Corsa EVO. Through coaching, community events, and competitive racing, SLR creates a home for drivers to grow, compete, and share their passion for motorsport.
